Egg Fried Rice | 蛋炒饭

Egg fried rice is a classic homemade dish
that is popular in many Asian households.
Growing up in China,
I often enjoyed egg fried rice as a quick and comforting meal.
Made with simple ingredients like rice, eggs, and green onions,
this recipe is easy to prepare and perfect for a busy day.

蛋炒饭是一道经典的家常菜，
在许多亚洲家庭中都很受欢迎。
在中国长大的我，从小就喜欢吃蛋炒饭，
因为它简单、美味，又让人感到温暖。
只需要米饭、鸡蛋、葱花等常见食材，
就能快速做出一道适合忙碌生活的美食。

Instructions | 制作步骤

  • Heat the cooking oil in a large pan over medium heat. 在大型平底锅中，用中火加热食用油
  • Add the beaten eggs and scramble until just cooked. 加入打散的鸡蛋，炒至半熟
  • Add the diced ham and stir-fry until it changes color. 加入切块的火腿，炒至变色
  • Add the cooked rice and stir-fry for 2~3 minutes. 加入熟的剩饭， 炒2-3分钟
  • Add the soy sauce and salt, then mix well. 加入酱油和盐，搅拌均匀
  • Add the chopped green onions and stir-fry for another minute. 加入葱花，再翻炒一分钟左右
  • Turn off the heat and serve the fried rice hot. 关火，趁热盛出炒饭
