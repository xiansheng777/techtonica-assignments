Egg Fried Rice | 蛋炒饭

Egg fried rice is a classic Chinese dish. It is simple, delicious, and easy to make. It is also a great way to use leftover rice.

蛋炒饭是一道经典的中国家常菜, 它制作简单且美味, 是合理利用剩饭的好方式之一

Photo credit: YTower Recipe

Ingredients | 食材

  • 2 cups of cooked rice | 2杯米饭
  • 2 eggs | 2个鸡蛋
  • A small amount of ham, diced into small pieces | 少许火腿，切碎
  • 2 teaspoons of cooking oil | 2汤勺食用油
  • 1 tablespoon of soy sauce | 1汤勺老抽
  • 2 green onions, chopped | 2根葱，切碎
  • Salt to taste | 适量盐

Instructions | 制作步骤

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a large pan over medium heat. 在大型平底锅中，用中火加热食用油
  2. Add the beaten eggs and scramble until just cooked. 加入打散的鸡蛋，炒至半熟
  3. Add the diced ham and stir-fry until it changes color. 加入切块的火腿，炒至变色
  4. Add the cooked rice and stir-fry for 2~3 minutes. 加入熟的剩饭， 炒2-3分钟
  5. Add the soy sauce and salt, then mix well. 加入酱油和盐，搅拌均匀
  6. Add the chopped green onions and stir-fry for another minute. 加入葱花，再翻炒一分钟左右
  7. Turn off the heat and serve the fried rice hot. 关火，趁热盛出炒饭
